# Skill test results, 2026-11-21

All tests ran on Sonnet 5.5 at high effort in clean sessions (no global rules, no hooks), on fresh
questions checked to appear nowhere in the skill files. Each pair was graded blind against a
checklist written before the run. One task per skill, so treat each result as directional.

| Skill | Test | Result | Installed now |
|---|---|---|---|
| learn-fast | old vs tstack rewrite vs old + owner's additions (3 tasks) | Owner picked the old version | Old version, plus "make it fun to read" and "show what matters" (key formulas and numbers stay on the main path) |
| research | old (171 lines) vs trimmed (83 lines), forced on (2 tasks) | Split 1-1; trimmed version 2-3x faster, half the cost | Trimmed version |
| grilling | with vs without, forced on | With won narrowly (9 vs 8), same time and cost | Unchanged |
| gtm | with vs without, forced on | With won clearly (8.5 vs 6): found that Slack already ships the feature, gave a stop rule; 483 s and $0.63 vs 22 s and $0.07 | Unchanged |
| find-buyers | with vs without, forced on | With won clearly (8 vs 4): 12 dated, linked developer posts vs a list from memory; 679 s and $0.43 vs 15 s and $0.07 | Unchanged |

## What the tests found

- The earlier learn-fast win on Terra was contaminated: the skill's own files held all three test
  topics. Keep test topics out of skill text, and leak-check every new test.
- In clean sessions none of these skills fire reliably on their own, not even research on
  "Research whether...". The router hook does the triggering in real use.
- Skills win where they bring something the model lacks (live evidence, the owner's taste) and tie
  where they restate what it already does (writing, studio, earlier tests).
